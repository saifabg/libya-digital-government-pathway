[MC: A commercial registration for an establishment] An electronic service provided by the Ministry of Commerce through the Saudi Business Center platform, allowing beneficiaries to start their businesses without having to visit service centers. / Access the platform: Via the National Unified Access through the Saudi Business Center platform. / ... Payment: Issue the invoice and pay the fees via SADAD or card. - After payment, the commercial registration is officially issued. / Automatic registration with relevant authorities: After issuing the record, registration is automatic with: the Ministry of Human Resources - the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority - the General Organization for Social Insurance - Saudi Post (National Address) - the Chamber of Commerce. / Duration of service / immediately / The service is provided in / Arabic and English / Service Fees / 500 / You can call the unified number 1900 / Service Delivery Channels / website / Branchs Link / Last Modified 16 Sep 2026
