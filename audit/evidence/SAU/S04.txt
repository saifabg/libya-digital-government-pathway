[Issue Family Card (Fathers) - Card] This service enables newly married citizens to request the issuance of a Family Card electronically with ease, without the need to visit Civil Affairs offices. The document is officially issued and delivered directly to the beneficiary's National Address, saving time and effort. / • The isue request must be for the first time only. / • The beneficiary must have at least one dependent (wife, son, or daughter). / • The beneficiary must not have any unpaid fines related to the Civil Affairs. / Beneficiaries / Citizens / Service Language / Arabic / English / Geographical Location / You can contact the nearest Ministerial Agency of Civil Affairs during working hours from 8 AM to 2 PM or reach them via the number (911) / Service channels / Absher Platform، Application, and Tawakkalna / Service Cost / 39.91 Riyal / Duration / Immediate
