[National ID Renewal Service - Card] This service enables the beneficiaries to renew their national identity card electronically using facial recognition technology, with the option to have the document delivered to the registered national address. / - The beneficiary cannot submit a new request while an existing request is still active. / - This service is available only when 180 days or less remain before the expiration of the National ID. / Beneficiaries / Citizens / Service Language / Arabic / English / Geographical Location / You can contact the nearest Ministerial Agency of Civil Affairs during working hours from 8 AM to 2 PM or reach them via the number (911) / Service channels / Absher Platform / Absher App / Tawakkalna App / Service Cost / Variable / Duration / Immediate
