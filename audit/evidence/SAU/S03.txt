[Renewing the Saudi passport - Card] This service allows Saudi citizens to easily renew their Saudi passports electronically without the need to visit passport offices. This can be done through simple and quick steps after submitting the application and completing the procedures via the Absher platform. / •Providing fingerprint and photo of the citizen in the Ministry of Interior's systems. / •Presence of the beneficiary inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. / •The beneficiary must have a valid passport. / •The photo registered in the Ministry of Interior's systems will be the one used in the issued passport. / Service Language / Arabic / English / Geographical Location / You can contact the nearest General Directorate of Passports office during working hours from 8 AM to 2 PM or reach them via the number (911). / Service channels / Absher Platform and Application / Service Cost / 300 SR for 5 years for each passport. / Duration / Immediate
