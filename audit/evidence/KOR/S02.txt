Mobile ID page: "※ 정부24를 통해 주민등록증 재발급 신청(IC포함) 시 주민센터를 직접 방문하여 증 교부를 받으신후에 모바일 주민등록증 발급을 하시기 바랍니다."
[Gloss: apply online or in person; 14 days; fee 5,000/10,000 won for reissue (first issue free); photo and old card required, family certificate checked by the official; online first-issue applicants must visit for fingerprints; the card is collected in person at the community centre.]
