ZAF S02 Smart ID card (first issue / re-issue) - South Africa
Accessed 2026-11-23
Source 1: https://ehome.dha.gov.za/ehomeaffairsv3 (eHomeAffairs landing page)
Extract: "eHomeAffairs allows the following: Submit ID and Passport applications online, Make online payments for applications, Make bookings where allowed. Note: (A branch visit is mandatory to capture and\or verify your biometric details, i.e. photo, fingerprints and signature). You can also finalise your application at any Home Affairs office; no appointment is required."
"Create profile, Capture your application, Attach supporting documents, Make payment, and Schedule appointments at Home Affairs enabled bank branches"
"Logon - Please enter your username (ID Number) and password ... A one-time-pin (OTP) will be sent to your cellphone"
Source 2: https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/identity-documents2 (via WebFetch): "Smart ID cards can only be applied for at modernised offices" (include banks, DHA offices in malls, mobile offices). First-time: "A certified copy of a birth certificate", "A certified copy of the parents, informant or legal guardian's ID", "No payment is required." Re-issue: copy of green ID/affidavit or old card; "The fee for the re-issue is R140.00." Collection at the chosen office after SMS.
Source 3: https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/id-smart-card/bank-branches: "Click the link to apply online and make a booking to visit your nearest Bank Branch." Banks: Capitec, Standard Bank, FNB, Absa.
Source 4: https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/id-smart-card/banks-digital: customers can "apply for Smart ID cards directly at selected bank branches"; "customers do not need to log onto eHomeAffairs to make bookings or payments"; "applications must be completed in person to allow for biometrics to be captured"; "Collections will be done at the bank branch where the application was made."; "You do not need to bring any documents."; "Banks may charge a convenience fee." First-time ID applications not available at bank branches (phase 1).
Source 5: National portal https://www.gov.za/services/services-residents/citizenship/personal-identification/apply-for-identity-document - information page linking out to DHA website, online booking (https://services.dha.gov.za/#/authenticate/identity) and bank list (https://www.dha.gov.za/banks); no application form on gov.za.
