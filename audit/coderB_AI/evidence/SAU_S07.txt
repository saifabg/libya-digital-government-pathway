SAU S07 Corporate income tax / zakat return filing and payment - Saudi Arabia
Accessed 2026-11-23
Source 1: ZATCA https://zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Pages/eServices-032.aspx ("Submit Corporate Income Tax (CIT) Return")
Extract: "This e-service allows you to file all related income tax returns for a specified period of time (including transaction pricing documents). Your tax return will be available to be filed on time at ZATCA portal."
Steps: Log into the ZATCA website; Select 'Returns'; Choose the return you want to submit; Fill in the return with your entity's financial data and submit it; You will receive a notification of the invoice amount via SMS and email.
Required Documents: "No Documents". Target Audience: All registered taxpayers for CIT. Service Duration: 5 Minutes. Service Cost: No Fees.
Service Channels (page markup): website = Yes; call center = No; branch = No; mobile app = No; relationship managers = No.
Payment Channels: SADAD = Yes; mada = Yes; Visa = Yes.
